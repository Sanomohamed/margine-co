    Template: ask buyers for their details

    Send this by WhatsApp or email before you issue the first invoice.

    Hi! To issue your e-invoice, please send:
1. Registered name (as per SSM / IC)
2. TIN (Tax Identification Number)
3. BRN or NRIC number
4. SST registration number (if any)
5. Business address
6. Phone and email
Thank you!
    Hai! Untuk mengeluarkan e-invois, sila hantar:
1. Nama berdaftar (mengikut SSM / IC)
2. TIN (Nombor Pengenalan Cukai)
3. No. BRN atau NRIC
4. No. pendaftaran SST (jika ada)
5. Alamat perniagaan
6. No. telefon dan emel
Terima kasih!

    Template: invoice data sheet

    MyInvois asks for around 55 data fields. Keep these ready for every sale, in a spreadsheet or your accounting software.

    SELLER (you, saved once)
- Registered name, TIN, BRN/NRIC
- MSIC business activity code
- SST registration no. (if registered)
- Address, phone, email

BUYER (per customer)
- Registered name, TIN, BRN/NRIC, SST no.
- Address, phone, email

INVOICE
- Your own invoice number (unique, never reused)
- Issue date and time
- Currency (MYR) and payment method

EACH LINE ITEM
- Description, classification code
- Quantity, unit price, discount
- Tax type and rate

TOTALS
- Total excluding tax, tax amount
- Total including tax, amount payable

    This kit is general information, not tax advice. LHDN has revised dates and thresholds several times, and sources disagree on when the penalty-free relaxation period ends. Confirm with the official LHDN e-Invoice guideline or your tax agent before relying on any date.
