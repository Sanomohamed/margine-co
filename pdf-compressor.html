PDF compressor

Shrink big PDFs, such as scans and photo-heavy files, so they are easier to email or upload. Your file is processed in your browser and is never uploaded.

Compression level

How it works: each page is turned into a compressed picture and put back into a new PDF. This works best on scans and photos. Because pages become pictures, text can no longer be selected or searched, and a PDF that is mostly plain text may not get smaller. If the result is bigger, keep your original.
